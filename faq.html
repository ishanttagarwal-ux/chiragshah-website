How do I book an initial consultation?
+
You can book by filling the contact form, calling +91 88980 50051, or messaging on WhatsApp. We respond within 2 hours and schedule appointments within 24-48 hours. Our AI assistant Lex is also available 24/7 for initial queries.
What areas of law does the firm handle?
+
Chirag Shah & Co. handles property litigation, divorce and family law, criminal cases, company law, cooperative society matters, money recovery, real estate and conveyance, consumer law, rent control, labour law, IT laws, intellectual property rights, and document registration.
Is the AI chatbot Lex confidential?
+
Yes. All conversations with Lex are encrypted and confidential within the session. Lex does not store personally identifiable information beyond your session unless you book a consultation. Formal attorney-client privilege applies when you engage our advocates directly.
What are your consultation fees?
+
Fees vary by case complexity. Our initial 30-minute consultation is available at a nominal fee. Please call +91 88980 50051 or WhatsApp us for current fee details applicable to your specific legal situation.
Where is the office located?
+
Our office is at 605, Pearl Plaza, Next to McDonald's, Opp. Andheri Railway Station, Andheri West, Mumbai - 400058. Easily accessible from Andheri Station (Western line), with parking nearby.
How long does a typical case take?
+
Duration varies significantly by type. Simple matters like document registration may be done in days. Litigation matters typically take months to years depending on court schedules and complexity. We provide realistic timelines at your initial consultation.
Do you offer payment plans?
+
Yes. We understand legal fees can be a concern and offer flexible payment arrangements for eligible clients. This is discussed at the initial consultation stage based on the nature and scope of your matter.
Can I get advice over phone or video call?
+
Yes. We offer consultations over phone, video call, and WhatsApp for clients who cannot visit in person. Contact us to schedule a remote appointment at your convenience.
What documents should I bring to my first appointment?
+
For property matters: title deeds, sale agreements, tax receipts. For family matters: marriage certificate, financial documents. For criminal matters: FIR copy, court notices. For company matters: MOA, AOA, board resolutions. Bring all correspondence related to your matter.
Do you handle urgent/emergency legal matters?
+
Yes. For urgent matters such as bail applications, injunctions or emergency restraining orders, call +91 88980 50051 directly. We prioritise emergency cases and aim to respond within the hour during office hours.
