Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I book an initial consultation?
+
You can book a consultation through our AI chatbot Lex, the AI voice call bot, or by filling out the contact form. We typically respond within 2 hours and schedule consultations within 24–48 hours.
What areas of law does the firm specialize in?
+
Chirag Shah & Co. handles property litigation, divorce and family law, criminal cases, company law, cooperative society matters, money recovery, real estate, consumer law, rent control, labour law, arbitration, IT laws, intellectual property and document registration.
Is the AI chatbot confidential?
+
Yes. All conversations with Lex are encrypted and confidential. Lex does not store personally identifiable information beyond the session unless you book a consultation. Formal attorney-client privilege applies when you engage our advocates directly.
What are your consultation fees?
+
Consultation fees vary based on the complexity and nature of the matter. Our initial 30-minute consultation is available at a nominal fee. Please contact us or ask Lex for current fee structures for your specific legal need.
